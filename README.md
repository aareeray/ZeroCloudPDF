# 🚀 pdfwithlove — Private & Local PDF & Image Tools

**pdfwithlove** is a privacy-first, browser-based document and image toolkit that runs **100% locally on your device**.  
No uploads. No servers. No tracking. No subscriptions.

Built for professionals who value **speed, security, and absolute data control**.

**63 tools** for PDFs and images, a **passport & visa photo maker** for 32 countries, and an **Android app** on Google Play.

Doc (WIP) - http://docs-pdfwithlove.netlify.app/
---

## 🎯 What is pdfwithlove?

Unlike traditional tools that upload your sensitive files to remote servers, **pdfwithlove processes everything directly in your browser** using modern **WebAssembly (WASM)** and **JavaScript**.

Your files **never leave your machine**.

## 🪪 Passport & Visa Photo Maker (New)

Make an official passport or visa photo **without uploading your face anywhere** — at [pdfwithlove.netlify.app/passport-photo](https://pdfwithlove.netlify.app/passport-photo/) and in the Android app.

### 📄 Documents
- **35 templates for 32 countries** — US, UK, Schengen, Canada, India, China, Germany, France, Japan, Australia and more
- Each template stores the official **size in millimetres, background colour, head height (chin to crown) and eye line**
- A requirements page per document, with a to-scale diagram, the rules to follow and FAQs

### ✂️ Editor
- **Automatic face centering** — the head is scaled into the allowed range and the eyes placed where the template wants them, in every browser
- **Background replacement on your device** — the wall is swapped for the colour the document asks for
- **Live checklist** — head height in mm, eye position, background and resolution checked as you drag and zoom
- Drag, pinch, zoom, straighten and rotate; phone camera or gallery; iPhone HEIC supported
- Exports at **exactly 300 DPI**, with the DPI written into the file so prints come out at the right size

### 🧰 Tools
- **Size makers** — 2×2 in, 35×45 mm, 33×48 mm, 30×40 mm, 40×60 mm, 4×4 cm, 1×1 in, 50×70 mm
- **Print sheets** — 4×6, 5×7, A4 and US Letter, as an **actual-size PDF** or JPEG (e.g. 6 US photos or 8 UK photos on one 4×6)
- **Compress to a KB limit** — the sharpest JPEG under 20 / 50 / 100 / 240 KB for online forms, without changing the pixel size
- **Head size checker** — measures chin-to-crown against the document's rule

## 🔐 Privacy & Security

### Absolute Data Sovereignty

#### 1. Local-First Processing
All operations execute **entirely in your browser’s memory (RAM)** using WASM and JS.  
Files are **never uploaded**, stored, or transmitted.

#### 2. No Persistence
- No databases  
- No cookies  
- No local storage  
- No cloud backups  

Once you close or refresh the tab, **everything is wiped from memory**.

## ✅ Security Best Practices
